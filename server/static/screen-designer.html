      How Jade screens are built
      
    
      
      
      
        
      Activities and the node tree
      
        
      A Jade screen is an activity (gui_activity_t): a tree of view nodes under
          act->root_node, a black fill that covers the display (below the status bar, if the activity has one).
          Nothing has an x/y position: each node gets a rectangle from its parent, and splits divide their rectangle among
          their children. To show the activity call gui_set_current_activity(act).
      
        
      The box model
      
        
      
          
      margins
            
      border
              
      padding
                
      content & children
      
              
      
            
      
          
      
        
      
        
      The rectangle is shrunk in this order: margins (gui_set_margins) leave a gap, the
          border (gui_set_borders) is painted inside the margins on the sides you choose, then
          padding (gui_set_padding) moves the content in. Fills and buttons paint only the area inside the
          padding. Margins and padding take one value (all sides), two (top/bottom, left/right) or four (top, right, bottom,
          left), from 0 to 255.

      How they combine
      
        
        
          
      • Rows and columns: a vsplit stacks children top to bottom, an hsplit puts them side by side.
            Nest them for grids: a vsplit of hsplits.
        • 
          
      • Header + body + footer: vsplit 20 / 55 / 25. Put a title in the first part, the content in the
            middle and a button row in the last. This is what add_title_bar() and
            make_show_message_activity() do.
        • 
          
      • Buttons with labels: a button paints its background and then its one child. Put a text in it, or a
            split for icon + text. Text inside a button switches to its selected colour when the button is selected.
        • 
          
      • Backgrounds: a fill paints a rectangle and then its one child on top. Use it behind a text you will update
            (so the old text is cleared), or with highlight to follow the user's theme.
        • 
          
      • Spacers: an empty vsplit with one part draws nothing. add_button() uses one for a
            button slot that has no event.
        • 
          
      • Exact sizes: switch a split to absolute for pixel sizes, and use fill remaining for the part
            that takes what is left.
        • 
          
      • Selection: the wheel/joystick moves between active buttons in screen order (top to bottom, then left to
            right); the first one, or the one marked initial selection, starts selected. A click posts
            GUI_BUTTON_EVENT with the button's event id, which gui_activity_wait_button() returns.
        • 
        
      
        
      Things the firmware does that may surprise you
      
        
        
          
      • Fills, buttons and pictures draw only their first child; a split draws at most as many children as it has parts.
        • 
          
      • Relative splits round down (size × value / 100), so a few pixels may stay empty at the end.
        • 
          
      • Text that does not fit wraps at the edge of its area; a line that does not fit vertically is not drawn. A
            scrolling text is drawn on one line instead.
        • 
          
      • Colour constants are RGB565 with the bytes swapped (TFT_RED is 0x00F8); the code
            below writes custom colours that way.
